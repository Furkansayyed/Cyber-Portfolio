Published Research Papers

Peer-reviewed publications in quantum authentication, entangled qubit protocols, and Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC).

IJESE Vol-13 Issue-5 · Apr 2025 Peer Reviewed

A Comprehensive Approach for Harnessing Entanglement for Next-Generation Authentication: From Passwords to Qubits

This paper investigates the vulnerabilities of conventional password-based and tokenized authentication schemes against quantum computational attacks. It presents a next-generation authentication architecture utilizing Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen (EPR) quantum entanglement. By leveraging quantum state collapse principles, any unauthorized interception inherently alters the quantum state, rendering spoofing physically impossible.

DOI: 10.35940/ijrte.A8345.14060326
Quantum Entanglement Next-Gen Authentication EPR Paradox Zero-Trust
IJIRT Vol-12 Issue-8 · Jan 2026 Peer Reviewed

Entangled Qubits and PQC: A Simulated Performance Analysis for Post-Quantum Authentication

A comparative performance and latency analysis between entangled qubit distribution schemes and contemporary Post-Quantum Cryptographic (PQC) lattice-based algorithms. Employs simulated distributed nodes to benchmark key generation latency, verification overhead, noise resilience, and eavesdropping detection thresholds under noisy quantum channel conditions.

Publication Link: IJIRT Vol-12 Issue-8
PQC Entangled Qubits Performance Simulation Channel Noise
IJCA Vol-187 Issue-105 · May 2026 Peer Reviewed

Zero-Trust Quantum Authentication for Distributed Systems using Device-Independent Protocols and Qiskit Simulation

Proposes a device-independent Zero-Trust quantum authentication framework for heterogeneous distributed nodes. Utilizing Bell-test violations and Python-based IBM Qiskit quantum circuit simulations, this study proves that authentication integrity can be cryptographically guaranteed even when the physical quantum devices themselves are untrusted or manufactured by third parties.

Simulation Code: 10.5120/ijcad7722d6000a6
